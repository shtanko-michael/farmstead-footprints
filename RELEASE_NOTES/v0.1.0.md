First release of **Footprints** by Farmstead.

- Footprints for players and 47 mobs, each with its own foot shape and a real gait.
- Prints in snow, sand, red sand, mud, gravel, soil, clay and soul sand, coloured from the ground and fading over time.
- Works with Sodium and Iris shader packs.
- Presets, an in-game settings screen, a commented config and `/footprints config|reload|stats`.
- 12 languages.
- Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21–1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge), 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge).
