First release of **Footprints** by Farmstead.

- Footprints for players and 47 mobs, each with its own foot shape and a real gait; modded mobs get feet from their own model.
- Two looks: detailed (a hollow and a lip) and pixel (the block's own texture pixels pressed in).
- Any soft ground takes prints, modded blocks too; prints crumble from the edges as they age, rain wears them, they go with a broken block.
- Water: boat wakes, rings where you wade, swim bubbles, damp prints on hard ground after water or rain; sparks under fast minecarts.
- Your own prints from PNG files, random variants, and shapes, textures and rules from resource packs.
- Settings with tabs, searchable block and mob lists, an About tab and "Try it"; presets; a commented config; `/footprints config|reload|stats`.
- Works with Sodium and Iris. No dependencies, not even Fabric API. 12 languages.
- Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge); 1.21–1.21.5, 1.21.7–1.21.11, 26.1–26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge).
